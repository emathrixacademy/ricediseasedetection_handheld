Handheld scanner

Pistol-grip body: cameras at the front, needle and syringe below, screen and buttons at the back, battery and solar panel on top.

How a scan works

  1. Point the scanner at the leaf and press SCAN.
  2. Both cameras (RGB and NoIR) photograph the same leaf from the same angle.
  3. The needle delivers the hydrogel and the ESP32 sweeps the electrodes for H2O2 and nitrate (about 20 s each).
  4. The Pi 5 runs the RGB and NIR models on the Hailo-8L and combines them with the biosensor reading by weighted late fusion.
  5. The result shows on the screen and is saved together with the images and the curves.
